BEFORE WE BEGIN

A clear scope.
An agreed price.

What affects the final price?

Property size, the number of floors, layout complexity, the quality of supplied plans and the interactive features you need.

Can I start with one house type?

Yes. Discuss a single model first, then agree the scope and price for additional house types.

Are branding and hosting included?

Branding, embedding, hosting arrangements, revisions and any ongoing charges are confirmed in your individual quotation.

How long does a project take?

Timing depends on the supplied references and scope. Include your preferred deadline in your brief so we can agree a realistic schedule.
