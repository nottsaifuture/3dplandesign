THE PROJECT

Familiar rooms.
A new perspective.

The model brings supplied floor plans and photographs together into one explorable property. Look at the complete exterior, separate the floors or step into the furnished interior.

Furniture and finishes follow the available reference photographs. Concealed details and room dimensions are approximate. This is an independent demonstration, not affiliated with or endorsed by the property’s owners or developers.

Explore it your way

  • Orbit, rotate and zoom
  • Ground, first-floor and cellar views
  • Dollhouse cutaway and exploded floors
  • Room viewpoints and approximate dimensions
  • Opening and closing doors
  • First-person walkthrough and mobile controls
  • Day / evening lighting, labels and minimap

A QUICK GUIDE

Make yourself at home.

01 / Look around

Drag to rotate. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Choose a floor, Dollhouse or a room from Viewpoint.

02 / Read the space

Turn on Dimensions to see approximate model room sizes in metres. Labels and the minimap help you orient yourself.

03 / Step inside

Choose Walkthrough. Use W/A/S/D and drag to look on desktop. On touch screens, use the joystick and swipe. Press E or Open / close door near a door.

For the most space on a phone, open the viewer in its own tab. Performance depends on your browser and device. If 3D is unavailable, the preview and project details remain accessible.
